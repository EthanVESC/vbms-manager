VBMS Manager @VERSION@ (beta)
=============================

A local app for VESC VBMS32 battery management systems: watch every cell,
set a charge limit, and schedule charging and balancing over WiFi.

!! READ THIS !!
This software was written by an AI (Claude, made by Anthropic). No human has
reviewed it. It manages lithium batteries connected to chargers in your home.
You use it entirely at your own risk, with no warranty. It may damage your BMS,
battery, charger or board, or cause a lithium battery fire. Keep checking your
boards with VESC Tool as usual. README.md has the full details.


Installing
----------
1. Drag "VBMS Manager" onto the Applications folder in this window.
2. Open it from Applications.

   This beta isn't notarized by Apple, so the first time macOS says it
   "can't be opened" or "can't verify the developer". Click Done (not
   Move to Trash), then:
     System Settings > Privacy & Security > scroll down to
     "VBMS Manager was blocked..." > Open Anyway > enter your password.
   You only need to do this once.

3. Your browser opens VBMS Manager. If it doesn't, click the battery icon in
   the menu bar (top right of the screen) and choose Open VBMS Manager.


How it runs
-----------
- VBMS Manager lives in the menu bar (battery icon). There's no Dock icon and
  no window: the app is the page in your browser at http://127.0.0.1:8642
- It starts by itself when you log in, so schedules and timers keep working.
  Turn that off with the menu's "Open at Login".
- Schedules, timers, recording and alerts only run while the app is running
  and the Mac is awake. If your Mac sleeps, they pause until it wakes.
- "Use from Phones on This Network" lets your phone open it too (view-only
  until you pair it with the code shown under Devices).
- Battery drain: while VBMS Manager is connected to a board, its BMS never
  goes to sleep and keeps powering itself from the pack. Over weeks this
  drains your battery (in practice a few percent per day). Left plugged in,
  the charger also kicks on for about 30 seconds roughly twice a day to top
  it back up. Before storing the board or leaving it for days, quit VBMS
  Manager or use Board > Free for VESC Tool so the BMS can sleep.
- Only one app can talk to a board at a time. While VBMS Manager is connected,
  VESC Tool can't connect to that board over WiFi. Use Board > Free for VESC
  Tool, or quit VBMS Manager from the menu.


Your data
---------
Everything VBMS Manager saves (boards, history, config backups, logs) is in
the hidden folder ~/.vbms_manager in your home folder. The menu's "Show Data
Folder" opens it. Nothing is sent anywhere except to your boards.
Config backups contain your WiFi key, so keep that folder private.

The app's own log is in ~/Library/Logs/VBMS Manager/ ("Show Log" in the
menu). Include it if you report a problem.


Uninstalling
------------
Quit it from the menu, drag it from Applications to the Trash, and delete
~/.vbms_manager if you don't want your history and backups.
