      Ask before every write

      The confirmation dialogs before charge limits, settings, live Pause/Allow and balancing commands, schedules and timers.

      Turn off write confirmations?

      ⛔ Read this first. With confirmations off, VBMS Manager stops asking before it changes your boards. One tap or click is enough:
      • Charge limits, charging and balancing settings and graph drags are written to the BMS's flash straight away.
      • The charging and balancing toggles pause, allow, stop or start immediately, including from a paired phone in your pocket.
      • Schedules and timers are saved and run without an approval step.
      • A slip of the finger or a wrong value can leave a lithium pack charging when you didn't mean it to, or not charging when you need it.

      Still asked: ⛔ danger changes (typed board name) and restoring a backup. Still enforced: every hard limit, backup, read-back and the change log, which marks writes made without confirmation.

      Only this computer can turn this on. Any paired device can turn it back on (confirmations on).
